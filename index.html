P-05

SPC control charts for QC lab test results

Synthetic data, illustrative spec limits

X-bar and R charts for three routine lab tests: growth promotion of media lots, WFI conductivity and autoclave load temperature. Limits are set on a 20-subgroup baseline and then used for monitoring. Five Nelson rules flag out-of-control points, every signal goes into a log with its investigation outcome, and Cp/Cpk is compared before and after.

FindingEach problem was flagged before any result failed spec. An upward drift in WFI conductivity was flagged the day after it began, and no reading ever went above 0.81 µS/cm against a 1.3 limit. An autoclave cold spot didn't show on the X-bar chart at all; only the R chart caught it, and Cpk dropped from 1.37 to 0.76.

JavaScript, Chart.js, statistical process control

P-06

Deviation and CAPA tracker

Synthetic data

A QA tracker for 167 deviations over 18 months across six areas of a device plant. It ages open records against a 30-day closure target, trends close-out time by month, finds root causes that repeat on the same equipment within 180 days, and checks whether each CAPA actually stopped the problem. There's also an Excel version built entirely on formulas, ready to keep adding records to.

FindingMedian days to close jumped from 16 to 36 once QA was short-staffed, and 7 of 13 open deviations are overdue. A line-clearance failure on one packaging line came back 37 days after its CAPA closed; its two CAPAs are the only failures among the 19 that have been checked.

JavaScript, Chart.js, Excel (COUNTIFS, array formulas, conditional formatting), Python

P-07

MicroQC Insight: LIMS data cleaning and QC dashboard

Simulated LabWare-style LIMS data

I built a QC micro data model in Power BI, pulled the raw tables back out of the .pbix file, and cleaned 4,706 EM and product release results in Python. The work covered profiling, de-duplication, decoding LIMS codes and rebuilding every OOS, alert and turnaround flag, which matched the Power BI model on every row. The rebuilt dashboard has a separate data integrity view.

Finding16.6% of results were reviewed by the analyst who entered them, and 41 audit-trail corrections fixed an impossible negative count. Two Power BI measures always showed 0% because they were built on empty columns.

Power BI, Python (pandas), JavaScript, Chart.js
