Is a Wave Coming? Reading Respiratory Viruses in Wastewater

Hundreds of sewage treatment plants test their wastewater for virus every week, and it doesn't depend on anyone getting tested or going to a doctor. I put every plant on a common scale in Python, then asked three questions for COVID-19, influenza A and RSV: does wastewater move before emergency department visits, how do the seasons compare, and can a simple model see the next few weeks coming?
