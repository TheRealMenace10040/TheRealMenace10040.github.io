6.0Method

All results are simulated for a portfolio demonstration and contain no real site data. Control limits use the standard Shewhart constants: X-bar limits are X̿ ± A₂R̄, R limits are D₃R̄ and D₄R̄. By default they are set from the first 20 subgroups (Phase I baseline) and held fixed for monitoring (Phase II), so a later shift shows up against the old limits instead of being absorbed into them. Nelson rules on the X-bar chart: 1, one point beyond 3σ; 2, nine in a row on one side of the centre; 3, six in a row steadily rising or falling; 5, two of three beyond 2σ on the same side; 6, four of five beyond 1σ on the same side. The R chart uses rule 1 only. Specification limits are illustrative: WFI conductivity uses the USP <645> Stage 1 limit at 25 °C, growth promotion uses a 70 to 130% recovery window and the autoclave uses a 121 to 124 °C exposure band.
