        What I Found While Cleaning

        • 779 results (16.6%) were reviewed by the analyst who entered them. That breaks second-person review, and no single analyst explains it.
        • 41 of the 101 audit-trail corrections changed a result from -1 to 0. A negative CFU count is impossible, so "-1" looks like a placeholder that slipped through entry.
        • "Late" depends on the rule. The 4-day SLA in the Power BI model flags 5.2% of results, but 43.2% missed their LIMS due date. Every one of those was an EM sample with a 3-day due date.
        • Two Power BI measures always showed 0%. Retest Rate and Lab Error Rate were built on columns that are completely empty.
        • The sample reviewer and result reviewer disagree on 3,889 records that share the same review timestamp. I treated the result-level reviewer as the record of truth.
