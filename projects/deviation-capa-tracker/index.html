6.0Definitions

All records are simulated for a portfolio demonstration and contain no real site data. Closure target: a deviation should be investigated and closed within 30 calendar days of being opened; an open record past 30 days is overdue. Days to close counts calendar days from opened to closed. CAPA is raised for every Major and Critical deviation, and for any Minor that repeats. CAPAs are due 90 days after the deviation closes, and get an effectiveness check 180 days after CAPA closure. Repeat: the same root cause on the same equipment or process within 180 days of the previous deviation. A repeat that opens after the prior CAPA was closed fails that CAPA's effectiveness check.
